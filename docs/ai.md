# AI Contribution Policy

This policy provides guidance for contributors and maintainers of IronCore regarding the use of Generative AI. This
document extends the [guidance provided by the Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/legal/generative-ai).

Contributors are free to use any tools they see fit for their contributions. But as a contributor **you are responsible
for what you submit**, regardless of the tool used to produce it. This includes the following responsibilities:
  - You must *review and understand your contribution*, and be able to explain it during review.
  - You should engage with other community members yourself, *not copy & paste replies from AI tools*.
  - You are the *author of the contribution* and must follow the [Contributing guidelines](/contributing), in
    particular sign the DCO.
  - You must ensure all contributions *comply with IronCore licensing*.

Maintainers will **judge contributions based on their quality**, not the tool used to create it.

Maintainers may **close or request rework of low-effort AI contributions** that don't meet our quality bar or shift the
review burden onto them.
